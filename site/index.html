Say no to cookies, nicely.

Cookie Away is a free Chrome extension that clicks “Reject” on cookie banners for you, even when it’s hidden three screens deep.

How it works

It finds the reject button so you don’t have to.

1

Finds the banner

It knows 27 consent tools by name, and reads the wording of the rest in about 20 languages.

2

Says no for you

No reject button? Kooky opens the settings, turns off what it can and saves your answer.

3

Checks it’s gone

After each click it checks the banner has gone from your screen. If it hasn’t, it has one more try.

Careful by design

Careful, not clever.

Never pays to say no

Some news sites only let you reject if you subscribe. Cookie Away leaves those banners for you to decide.

You keep your place

Cookie Away never clicks a link that takes you off the page, so you carry on reading what you came for.

Knows when to stop

If a banner keeps coming back, it pauses on that site for 24 hours instead of looping.

Off when you want it

Turn it off for any site from the popup.

Install

Private by default.

No account, no server, no analytics. Your settings stay in your browser, and the code is open for anyone to read.

  1. Download the code and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder. Kooky appears in your toolbar.

Cookie Away is in review on the Chrome Web Store. Once it’s approved, this becomes one click.

Open source

Fewer banners to fight, and code you can read.

Still seeing a banner on a site you use? Send us the link. You’re free to read, fork and change the code under the MIT licence.

Questions

Good questions.

Does Cookie Away send my browsing anywhere?

No. Cookie Away has no server and no analytics, and your settings stay in your browser. The only exception is the optional Jev feature, which is off by default, needs your own API key, and sends only the site’s name, the banner text and its button labels.

How is rejecting different from hiding the banner?

Hiding a banner doesn’t tell the site you said no, and some sites keep tracking until you answer. Cookie Away actually answers, by clicking reject or switching everything off in the settings and saving.

What does Cookie Away do on pay-or-accept cookie walls?

Cookie Away leaves pay-or-accept walls alone and doesn’t click anything on them. On those sites rejecting cookies means paying for a subscription, and the extension never pays for anything or makes that choice for you, so you decide whether to accept, subscribe or leave.

Which browsers does Cookie Away work in?

Chrome, and other Chromium browsers such as Edge, Brave and Arc. It’s in review on the Chrome Web Store; until then you can install it from GitHub in about a minute.

Does Cookie Away reject every cookie banner?

No, Cookie Away doesn’t reject every cookie banner, and its test results are published so you can check that for yourself. When you find a banner it didn’t answer, send us the link to the site, because every report makes the next version better.
